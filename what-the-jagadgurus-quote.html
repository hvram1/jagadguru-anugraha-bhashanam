What this page does not claim

Measured, mostly not yet heard

Every span here was placed by forced alignment against the audio and re-derived from the aligned word stream before printing. That is weaker than listening. Thirteen of these 41 talks were verified by ear in August; the rest have not been heard.

Two talks whose counts answer a different question

Greatness of Sanskrit is delivered in Sanskrit throughout, so the script gate makes candidates of the Jagadguru's own prose — its 153 unnamed passages are not missing texts, and it is excluded from the totals. Misconceptions Dispelled is in Kannada and only its first four minutes carry Sanskrit in Devanagari; the rest was never searched.
